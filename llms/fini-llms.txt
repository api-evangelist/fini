# Fini AI

> Self-improving AI agent for enterprise customer support. Resolves 90% of support tickets with 99% accuracy across voice, chat, and email. Purpose-built for fintech, banking, healthcare, insurance, and other regulated industries where compliance is non-negotiable.

Fini is a multi-modal AI agent that understands customer intent, retrieves the right data, and takes action. Live in 14 days, fully autonomous in 30. Backed by Y Combinator (S22) and Matrix Partners. Used by Atlas, Wefunder, ICICI Bank, PostFinance, Acorns, CoverGenius, Unit, Column Tax, Qogita, Found, Bitdefender, DistroKid, LISA Hockey, TrainingPeaks, and US Chamber of Commerce.

Core architecture is RAGless retrieval combined with the Fini Agent Loop, Knowledge Atlas (self-updating AI help center), and Agentic Actions. The Era 2 positioning is "agents maintaining agents," where Fini detects its own knowledge gaps, learns from real resolutions, and keeps getting sharper without human re-tuning. Zero-Pay Guarantee: 90% resolution in 90 days, or you pay nothing.
